## Deep Dive

The Metroid series has enjoyed an extraordinary renaissance over the past five years. When *Metroid Dread* released on the original Switch in October 2021, it shattered franchise sales records by selling over three million units worldwide, proving conclusively that high-budget, side-scrolling 2D Metroidvanias still command enormous commercial appeal. With *Metroid Ravenous*, Nintendo is leaning hard into that momentum, combining atmospheric horror, kinetic movement, and intricate labyrinthine map design.

From a technical perspective, moving the 2D Metroid framework onto the Nintendo Switch 2 opens up huge possibilities for environmental interaction and visual fidelity. Early preview detail indicates that *Metroid Ravenous* leverages real-time lighting physics, fluid particle simulations, and hyper-responsive combat frames that target a locked 60 frames per second—with rumor of a 120Hz high-frame-rate mode for supported displays. The game’s title implies a sinister thematic shift, focusing on predatory alien ecosystems that actively adapt to Samus’s arm-cannon upgrades and suit enhancements.
