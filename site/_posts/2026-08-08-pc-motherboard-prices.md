The PC hardware industry has been experiencing a series of price hikes and shortages, starting with the RAMageddon in 2018, where RAM prices skyrocketed due to high demand and limited supply. The price hike is expected to affect all types of motherboards, from budget-friendly options to high-end gaming motherboards.

## Deep Dive
From a technical perspective, the potential price hike in PC motherboards can be attributed to the increasing complexity of modern motherboards. The latest motherboards support high-speed interfaces such as USB 3.2 Gen 2, SATA 6Gb/s, and PCIe 4.0, which require more advanced components and manufacturing processes. Additionally, the growing demand for Wi-Fi 6 and high-speed LAN has led to the inclusion of more expensive components, such as Wi-Fi 6 modules and high-speed LAN controllers. These advancements in technology have driven up production costs, making it more expensive to manufacture high-quality motherboards.

The price hike may also be influenced by the current global semiconductor shortage, which has affected the production of various components, including CPUs, GPUs, and motherboard chipsets. The shortage has led to increased prices for these components, which in turn has driven up the cost of manufacturing motherboards. Furthermore, the ongoing trade tensions and tariffs imposed on imported components have also contributed to the increase in production costs. The combination of these factors has created a perfect storm that may lead to a price hike in PC motherboards.

In terms of market data, the PC motherboard market has been growing steadily over the past few years, driven by the increasing demand for gaming PCs and high-performance computing. However, the potential price hike may affect this growth, as buyers may be deterred by the increasing costs.
